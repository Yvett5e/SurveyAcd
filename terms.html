Terms & Privacy Policy

Purpose

This survey is being conducted as part of an academic project, which may also be submitted to an academic or student competition. It is not being run for any commercial or marketing purpose, and no individual response will be sold or given to advertisers.

What data is collected

  • Basic background information (name, age, education level, country, state, income range, gender)
  • Device type (detected automatically, not asked)
  • Answers to a set of arithmetic, puzzle, and short task questions
  • The background color shown during each task question, and how long each question took to answer
  • A few short lifestyle questions (weather, mood, taste preference)

How the data will be used

  • Responses will be used to analyze patterns for this project (for example, examining whether background color affects how people answer certain questions).
  • Individual, identifiable responses (such as your name or specific answers tied to you) will not be shared with anyone outside the project, including competition judges or reviewers.
  • Aggregate or summary results — such as averages, overall trends, or group comparisons — may be shared publicly or with competition judges as part of presenting this project. These summaries will not identify individual participants.

Voluntary participation

  • Participation is entirely voluntary. You may leave the survey at any point without penalty; incomplete entries will be discarded.
  • You may skip optional questions (such as income).

Data storage

Responses are stored securely and are only accessible to the person conducting this project. Data will be retained only as long as needed to complete the project and will be deleted afterward.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this survey or how your data is used, please contact anna2807ku@gmail.com.

By checking the consent box at the start of the survey, participants confirm they have read this notice and agree to take part on these terms.
